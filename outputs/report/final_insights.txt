========== BITCOIN SENTIMENT ANALYSIS — FINAL INSIGHTS ==========
Date Range: May 2023 → May 2025
Total Trades Analyzed: 207,327

------------------------------------------------------------------
1. PERFORMANCE BY SENTIMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Extreme Greed → Highest avg PnL ($66.11) and win rate (46.24%)
- Fear          → Avg PnL ($53.50), win rate (41.98%)
- Extreme Fear  → Lowest avg PnL ($31.27) and win rate (36.90%)
- Greed         → Avg PnL ($42.43), win rate (38.81%)

CONCLUSION: Traders perform 111% better in Extreme Greed vs Extreme Fear.

------------------------------------------------------------------
2. LEVERAGE BEHAVIOR
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Extreme Greed → Avg leverage proxy: 953.50 (highest)
- Extreme Fear  → Avg leverage proxy: 430.18 (lowest)
- Greed         → Avg leverage proxy: 895.41
- Fear          → Avg leverage proxy: 408.72

CONCLUSION: Traders take 2.2x more leverage during Greed vs Fear.

------------------------------------------------------------------
3. HIGH LEVERAGE PATTERN
------------------------------------------------------------------
- High leverage during Fear  → Avg PnL: $115.25
- High leverage during Greed → Avg PnL: $105.09

CONCLUSION: Surprisingly, high leverage trades yield MORE during Fear.
            This suggests experienced traders use high leverage selectively
            during Fear when prices are undervalued.

------------------------------------------------------------------
4. LEVERAGE VS PNL CORRELATION
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Correlation: 0.0167 (near zero)

CONCLUSION: Leverage size alone does NOT predict PnL.
            Strategy and timing matter more than leverage amount.

------------------------------------------------------------------
5. TRADE FREQUENCY
------------------------------------------------------------------
- Most trades happen during Fear (61,011) and Greed (49,127)
- Least trades during Extreme Fear (21,235)

CONCLUSION: Traders are most active during moderate sentiment,
            and pull back during Extreme Fear.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STRATEGY SUGGESTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BEST TIME TO TRADE   → During Greed or Extreme Greed phases
2. LEVERAGE CONTROL     → Keep leverage low during Extreme Fear
3. OPPORTUNISTIC PLAY   → High leverage selectively during Fear
                          can yield above average returns
4. AVOID               → High frequency trading during Extreme Fear
                          (lowest win rate at 36.90%)
5. RISK CONTROL        → Win rate never exceeds 47% in any condition
                          Always use stop loss regardless of sentiment
